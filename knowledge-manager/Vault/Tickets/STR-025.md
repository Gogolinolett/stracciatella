# Problem

Should the camera freeze during a reaction delay, and why does freezing read as stop-motion?

# Lösung

Decision: while a deferred-action reaction delay is in flight, the bot camera keeps easing toward its last aim point - aimCameraAt records the point and the deferred gate replays it each tick. Movement keys stay released, but the gaze finishes its swing and keeps saccading. Alternatives: freeze everything during the delay (original) pro: simplest, and the pause is clearly visible; contra: the spring-damper camera can be mid-swing when the delay starts, since SCANNING 15 deg facing tolerance fires while the camera still has high angular velocity, so halting the turn for 1-10 ticks and then resuming reads as stop-motion, the opposite of the humanness the delay exists for. Ease toward the last aim point during the delay (chosen) pro: the body pauses with movement released while the gaze settles naturally, exactly what a human pausing between deciding and acting looks like, and saccades stay alive; contra: one extra static field set (lastAim) on every camera-driving tick. Only delay transitions where the camera has converged pro: no mid-swing freeze possible; contra: couples delay placement to camera state, and SCANNING to NAVIGATING with its 15 deg tolerance is precisely the transition where the beat is most visible and most wanted. Rationale: the reaction delay models a decision pause, not a motor freeze - humans stop walking while they think, but their eyes finish settling on the target. See also [[STR-042]].
