# Problem

The bot does not start mining even when it looks at the right block - why does human-aim jitter break the hit-result gate?

# Lösung

Decision: human-aim offsets are still applied, but only on the two axes perpendicular to the face normal - the jitter is clamped to the face plane. Problem: adding an offset along the face normal pushes the aim point off the face plane, so the raycast exits the block face range by the offset amount and just barely grazes a neighbour, or the platform below a single block. The hit-result gate then sees the neighbour and the bot stares forever, reported as the bot does not start mining even when it looks at the right block. Rationale: restricting the jitter to the in-face axes keeps the visual humanization while guaranteeing the aim ray crosses the target face rather than skirting it. See also [[STR-032]] and [[STR-033]].
