# Problem

The camera whips around when the player steps past a path node at very short range - why does the desired yaw flip by up to 180 degrees?

# Lösung

Decision: stableDesiredYaw(aimDx, aimDz) returns the atan2 yaw toward the aim vector but freezes at its last stable value while the horizontal distance is below 0.5 blocks. It is used for the main movement desired-yaw and for the landing-brake retarget, and is reset on start(). Alternatives: raw atan2 every tick (original) pro: no state; contra: within about 0.5 blocks of a node, stepping past the point flips the yaw target by up to 180 deg between ticks, and the camera whips after every flip - a fast sweep with the velocity cap, a snap without it. This happens at overflown intermediate nodes where airborne advance is denied at sharp turns, during landing-brake slides toward a close node, and it was the engine of the post-landing pirouette. Freeze below 0.5 blocks (chosen) pro: the gaze keeps its last meaningful direction through the unstable zone, node advance or genuine separation resets it naturally, and it costs one float plus one boolean of state; contra: while frozen the gaze can be slightly stale if the player drifts sideways, which is irrelevant at sub-half-block range where any direction reads as looking at the node. Slerp or low-pass on the desired yaw pro: smooths flips too; contra: adds lag to all target changes including legitimate course corrections, whereas the instability is strictly a near-field problem so a distance gate is the precise tool. Rationale: the yaw target is simply not meaningful information at sub-half-block range - any human looks at the block underfoot without re-aiming at its mathematical center point.
