# Problem

Every gallery face lies below the eye line and the gallery ceiling blocks the ray while the player is still on the staircase - when may ore be chased?

# Lösung

Decision: the scan-and-chase step is gated on the player block Y equalling tunnelFeetY. Until then the behavior digs the next tunnel slice, whose drops pull the player down to level via the collect-walk. Evidence: per-failure look diagnostics, for example player at y -59.00 with hitResult at y -59, showed the remaining failure mode - with the player still on the staircase, every gallery face lies below his eye line and the gallery ceiling blocks the ray, so galleries are only workable while standing at tunnelFeetY. Rationale: this is also just what strip mining is - you work veins from your tunnel, not hanging off the staircase. See also [[STR-049]] and [[STR-052]].
