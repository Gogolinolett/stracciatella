# Problem

Every pause the bot makes is 1-10 ticks, so over minutes the cadence is recognizably machine-constant - where should longer pauses go?

# Lösung

Decision: the reaction delay at the SCANNING to NAVIGATING transition is drawn via HumanBehavior.randomTaskSwitchDelayTicks - with probability longPauseChance (default 0.04) a uniform breather pause of longPauseMinTicks to longPauseMaxTicks (default 12-25 ticks) replaces the standard Gaussian reaction delay. Other transitions keep the standard delay. Alternatives: no long pauses (original) pro: no test-budget impact; contra: every pause is 1-10 ticks so the cadence is recognizably machine-constant over minutes of watching, and the module description had promised pause patterns since the start while only the per-transition Gaussian existed. Long-pause chance on every scheduleAction pro: most variety; contra: multiplies the flake risk in tick-budgeted tests, since the within-reach vein flow alone rolls twice or more per run, and a mid-vein stall reads less natural than a pre-walk one. Long-pause chance only at SCANNING to NAVIGATING (chosen) pro: the spotted it, about to walk over moment is where humans genuinely idle by re-gripping the mouse or glancing at inventory, and walk-flow tests have the largest timeout budgets of 200-280 ticks so a rare extra 25 ticks fits; contra: the breather never occurs while standing in a vein, which is acceptable since the pre-attack hesitation and reaction delays already vary that cadence. Idle fidgeting with random look-arounds while IDLE pro: strongest idle realism; contra: IDLE means no task, which is out of scope for task-execution humanness and can be layered later. Rationale: applying it at a single transition gives a visible cadence-breaker with bounded, testable timing risk. The defaults are deliberately conservative at 4 percent and at most 25 ticks, and both knobs are config-exposed. See also [[STR-042]].
