# Problem

Enqueuing a whole gallery or staircase step as one batch causes repeated look timeouts and 400-tick break timeouts - how should dig work be queued?

# Lösung

Decision: steps queue follow-up batches (batchQueue) that execute strictly one after another with verification and collect-walk in between, instead of one bulk plan. A gallery is one column per batch with the ore as the final batch, and a staircase step is ceiling plus head first, then the foot. Problem and evidence: enqueuing the whole gallery or a whole staircase step as one batch failed in testing because the controller nearest-task selection reorders the tasks by distance, targeting blocks farther down the gallery while they are still hidden behind unmined columns - 9 look timeouts on a single head block in one run - and stair feet additionally triggered 400-tick server-rejected break timeouts when aimed at their covered top face. Rationale: the pause between batches is the same dig a bit, step in, dig on rhythm a human shows, so the fix costs nothing in humanness. Note the interaction with nearest-task selection: greedy nearest-from-player ordering is correct for independent targets but actively harmful for a sequence whose visibility depends on execution order, which is why the sequencing must come from the behavior rather than the queue.
