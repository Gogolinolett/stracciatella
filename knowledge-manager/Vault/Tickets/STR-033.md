# Problem

With hit-result gating in place the bot locks up in LOOKING when mining a block in the middle of a stack - where should it aim?

# Lösung

Decision: aim at the center of the face most directly visible from the bot eye, computed via BlockInteractor.faceTowardPlayer, instead of the block center. Problem: with hit-result gating in place, aiming at the block center became unworkable for blocks in the middle of a stack, for example mining a tree from top to bottom. The center of the top log sits behind the lower log near face from the bot perspective, so a raycast aimed at the center lands on the lower log and the hit-result gate locks the bot in LOOKING until lookTimeout. Why the face aim works: the aim point shifts by 0.5 either way along the face-normal axis, so the raycast clears intermediate blocks and lands on the target. faceTowardPlayer already picks the same dominant-axis face used in the destroy packet, so no new concept is introduced. See also [[STR-032]] and [[STR-029]].
