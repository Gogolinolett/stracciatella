# Problem

The camera still orbits the target for a few ticks between landing and the arrival speed gate opening - why does the arrival disc plus speed gate alone not remove the pirouette?

# Lösung

Decision: when on the ground inside the arrival disc while the speed gate is still closed, skip target steering entirely - hold the camera yaw (updateYaw(getYaw()), so the spring just settles) and run the same 8-way applyCounterBrake the landing brake uses, until the gate opens and arrival fires. Evidence: per-tick camera traces showed that during the 1-4 ticks between landing (speed 0.15-0.26) and the gate opening (at most 0.12) the normal movement logic kept steering toward the walk target. Standing next to the point, the desired yaw orbited it at roughly 7 deg per tick (desiredYaw -150.7 then -143.5 then -136.5 then -125.7 over four ticks at a constant 0.2 speed) and the camera followed. The S-only shouldBrake path pushes opposite the view, which points at the target, so the sideways momentum component was never killed and the orbit persisted. Verified by trace afterwards: zero remaining ticks of target-steering inside the disc, and the camera yaw stays constant through landing and stop. See also [[STR-006]] and [[STR-008]].
