# Problem

The vertical aim is identical every tick during walks and reads as a frozen bot gaze - how is pitch variance added without delaying jumps?

# Lösung

Decision: while straightWalkTicks is above 20, the bot is on the ground, not approaching a jump, and the target is more than 1.5 blocks away, add a slowly-refreshing offset (2.5 deg Gaussian either way, refreshed every 20-40 ticks) to the desired pitch passed into CameraController.updatePitch. Outside those conditions the offset is zeroed so the next walk starts clean. Alternatives: no pitch variance (state before the change) pro: most predictable, pitch always near horizontal; contra: frozen gaze, the vertical aim is identical every tick of every walk and reads as bot. Continuous pitch noise pro: most lively; contra: when approaching a jump the pitch is part of the camera-derived facing target, so noisy pitch makes the JUMP_FACING_TOLERANCE_DEG angular check flutter and can delay a jump by 1-2 ticks, manifesting as occasional stuck at the edge. Gated low-frequency offset (chosen) pro: the 20-tick straight-walk gate guarantees the bot is well past any jump prep, the 1.5-block target distance gate excludes the precise approach, and because the offset is part of the desired pitch the spring-damper does the smoothing so the output is a slow drift rather than jitter; contra: the offset must be zeroed on exit, and failing to do so would leave the next jump approach with a stale tilt. Rationale: pitch variance is a scanning-the-path cue that only makes sense during sustained walking. The straight-walk counter is shared with micro-strafing for the same reason - once the bot is committed to a long straight run, both humanness effects activate together. See also [[STR-010]].
