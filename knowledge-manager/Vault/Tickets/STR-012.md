# Problem

Long jumps fire instantly the moment conditions align and read as a script - where should a pre-jump pause go?

# Lösung

Decision: when entering the gap of 5 or more retreat branch from a standstill (forwardVel at most 0.14), pause for a Gaussian 4-12 tick window (mean 7, sigma 2) before starting the retreat. The camera continues aiming at the target during the pause and no movement keys are pressed. If the bot enters with sprint-speed velocity (forwardVel above 0.14, meaning the standard skip-retreat branch applies), the hesitation is skipped as well. Alternatives: no hesitation (state before the change) pro: fastest jump execution; contra: long jumps fire instantly when conditions align and read as a script, whereas a human pauses to look at a 4-air-block gap before committing. Hesitation on all jumps from gap 2 upward pro: most consistent humanness; contra: adds 250-500 ms to every step-up and short jump, chain-jumps over single-block platforms get visibly choppy, and tests relying on a continuous gap=2 chain regress. Hesitation on gap 5 or more gated to a standstill (chosen) pro: targets exactly the situation a viewer notices, the bot facing a wide gap and stopping to pre-aim, while mid-stride entries that are already sprinting do not pause so smooth gap-2 chains are preserved; contra: adds roughly 250-500 ms per max-range jump. Look-then-pause via camera-only state pro: hesitation visible without halting movement; contra: the retreat phase needs the bot still, and entering retreat at speed risks falling off the back edge - stopping is the correct mechanic regardless of humanness. Rationale: it matches the visual cue a human gives at a long jump and is mechanically aligned with the retreat phase, which already requires the bot to stop.
